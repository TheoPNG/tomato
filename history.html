Past Productions

Highlights from our shows and programs in 2022, 2023, and 2024.

2022

Tomato: The Musical

Plot Summary: Tomato: The Musical follows a young tomato who is forced to decide whether he is a fruit or a vegetable. As he meets characters from both sides, he learns he does not need to choose one group to belong. With help from friends, the fruit-and-vegetable feud is resolved and everyone comes together.

What Tomato Means to Us: This was Tomato Theatre Company's first production and the show that inspired our name. It proved we could build meaningful, youth-led theatre and make a real impact in our community.

"It was really rewarding to see my hard work and ambition show through this wonderful musical." - Adora Bricher

2023

A Very Pirate Musical

Plot Summary: A young pirate captain and crew search for treasure, but their carelessness pollutes the ocean. With courage from Calico, pirates and sea creatures finally communicate, repair the harm, and clean up the sea together.

"It was amazing to see our vision grow as we expanded our camp this year." - Tessa McNees

2024

Tomato Theatre Fundraiser

Last year we hosted our first fundraiser with an auction, raffle, and performance featuring many team members and returning campers. The event was a huge success thanks to generous support from our community, and we surpassed our donation goal.
